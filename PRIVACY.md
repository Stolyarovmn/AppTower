# Privacy Policy — AppTower

Effective date: 9 September 2026

AppTower is a browser extension that organises websites selected by the user
into a side panel, a compact rail, groups, templates and workspaces.

## Data stored by the extension

AppTower stores its configuration in browser extension storage: workspace names,
shortcut URLs and titles, groups, templates, appearance preferences, per-site
settings and enabled modules. This data stays in the browser profile.

Browser sync is optional and disabled by default. When you enable it, the
configuration selected for sync is stored through the browser's sync service and
associated with your signed-in browser account. AppTower does not sync cookies,
passwords, form entries, page content or browsing history.

## Network and page access

AppTower has no own server, analytics, advertising, telemetry or data sale. It
does not send the stored configuration to AppTower or a third party.

The extension can run its compact rail on HTTP(S) pages and load websites that
you explicitly open or pin. It may request the web app manifest of a visited
site to identify it, and it uses browser-provided favicons for shortcuts. Those
requests go to the site involved. The websites you open remain subject to their
own policies.

AppTower does not read or transmit page content, credentials, cookies or form
data. Browser permission prompts remain controlled by the browser.

## Your choices

You can edit or delete shortcuts and settings from AppTower, disable optional
sync, export your configuration, or remove the extension. Removing the extension
removes its locally stored data according to the browser's normal extension-data
behaviour.
